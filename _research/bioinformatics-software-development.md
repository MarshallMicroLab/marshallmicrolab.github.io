## Overview

We build bioinformatics tools and workflows that turn recurring analyses into reusable, tested, and documented research software. Development priorities include accessibility, provenance, and reproducibility.

## Objectives

- Translate research methods into maintainable software.
- Automate quality checks and provenance tracking.
- Share documentation and examples that support reuse.
