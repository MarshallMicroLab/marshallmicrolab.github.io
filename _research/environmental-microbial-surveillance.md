## Overview

Environmental microbial surveillance combines sampling design, molecular methods, genomic analysis, and spatial context. This broad research area examines how these approaches can characterize microbial presence and diversity across environments.

## Objectives

- Develop reproducible approaches for environmental sequence data.
- Evaluate spatial and temporal patterns while accounting for sampling design.
- Connect laboratory, computational, and geographic workflows.
