## Overview

This research area links genomic observations with temporal, geographic, and epidemiological metadata. The goal is to develop careful, reproducible approaches for evaluating microbial relatedness and patterns of spread.

## Objectives

- Compare genomic diversity across locations and sampling periods.
- Integrate sequence data with relevant epidemiological metadata.
- Communicate uncertainty and analytical limitations clearly.
