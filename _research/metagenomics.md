## Overview

Our metagenomics work uses culture-independent sequencing to characterize microbial communities in host-associated and environmental samples. Analyses emphasize quality control, appropriate controls, and reproducible interpretation.

## Objectives

- Characterize taxonomic and functional diversity in microbial communities.
- Evaluate workflows for short- and long-read metagenomic data.
- Develop clear reporting practices for complex sequencing studies.
