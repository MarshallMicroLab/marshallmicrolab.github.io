## Overview

We develop and apply genome-based approaches for characterizing microbial populations. Projects may integrate short- and long-read sequence data with reproducible computational analysis.

## Objectives

- Describe genomic variation within and among microbial populations.
- Evaluate analysis methods for complete and draft genomes.
- Build transparent workflows that support reproducible interpretation.
